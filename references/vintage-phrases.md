# Vintage Phrases — 1930s Radio Baseball Vocabulary

A menu to draw from. Don't copy the phrases verbatim every time — rotate, combine, improvise in the same register. The goal is to make every call feel drawn from this bin without any single phrase becoming a tic.

## Nicknames for the ball

- the horsehide
- the old apple
- the pill
- the pellet
- the onion
- the rock (more 1950s, use sparingly)
- the leather (often for the glove though)
- the spheroid
- the ol' pill

## Nicknames for the bat

- the lumber
- the timber
- the club
- the willow
- the stickwork ("he does his stickwork from the left side…")
- the bat-stick

## Positions / players

- **Pitcher:** twirler, slabman, hurler, portsider (if left-handed), chucker, the man on the hill, fireballer, moundsman
- **Catcher:** backstop, receiver, man behind the plate, the mask
- **First baseman:** first sacker, the initial sack, keystone (no — that's 2B)
- **Second baseman:** keystone sacker, second sacker, middle infielder
- **Shortstop:** shortpatch man, shortstopper, the short field
- **Third baseman:** hot-corner man, third sacker, the pepperbox
- **Outfield:** gardener, pasturer, flychaser ("left gardener", "right fielder", "centerfielder")
- **Batter:** stickman, hitter, swinger, the man at the dish, the batsman

## Calling balls and strikes

- "He checks the sign… the stretch… and delivers — *outside*, ball one."
- "Swung on and missed! Strike one."
- "Called strike on the corner! That was a *beauty* of a pitch."
- "Ball two, low and inside."
- "Fouled off, down the left-field line. The count holds at 1-and-2."
- "Strike three, called! He watched it go right on by him!"
- "Oh my, he *fanned* him! Swung through a third strike!"

## Hits

- **Single:** "a base hit into right!", "slashes one through the hole", "a clean single", "a little looper in front of the center fielder", "ribbon line drive into left"
- **Double:** "a two-bagger off the wall", "rolls into the corner for a double", "into the gap — that'll be a stand-up two-bagger", "off the fence, Frazier pulls into second"
- **Triple:** "three-bagger! he's really moving now!", "all the way to the triangle in deep right-center", "that ball rolls and *rolls*"
- **Home run:** see below
- **Infield hit:** "beat it out! a real hustle play", "leg hit, he was running all the way"

## Home-run calls (use the biggest vocabulary here)

- "Going, going, GONE! Into the bleachers in right field!"
- "A towering drive! Back, back, back goes Tatis — he's at the warning track — he's at the wall — and *that ball is OUT OF HERE!*"
- "Fly me to the moon! That ball is in *orbit*!"
- "Oh, did he get ahold of that one! A mighty swing — deep to left — *adios*!"
- "It's a four-bagger! A circuit clout! A round-tripper!"
- "A moon shot! Into the upper deck!"
- "He got *all* of it! Goodbye, baseball!"
- "That ball was *murdered*. Straight into the center-field bleachers."
- "Tape-measure job, folks. They'll be talking about that one tomorrow."

## Outs

- **Strikeout:** "fanned him!", "struck him out swinging / looking", "punched him out", "got the K", "threw three by him", "sat him down"
- **Groundout:** "grounds it to short — throw across — in time, one away", "easy grounder, right at the second sacker", "chopper to third, picked up, throw over, he's out"
- **Flyout:** "fly ball, routine, to center — got it", "high pop to the catcher, drifting toward the stands — caught in foul ground", "lazy fly to right, into the glove"
- **Lineout:** "line shot — right at the shortstop! caught! saved a base hit right there"
- **Double play:** "around the horn! two! two out of one! beautiful!"
- **Force play:** "he gets the force at second, but the throw to first is late"

## Walks, HBPs, miscellaneous

- **Walk:** "free pass", "base on balls", "ball four, take your base", "drew the walk", "earned the free trip"
- **Hit by pitch:** "he *plunked* him!", "a brushback that caught him on the elbow", "wore that one", "he's taking first, rubbing his shoulder"
- **Wild pitch:** "the pitch got away from the catcher — and the runner takes second!"
- **Balk:** "the umpire's calling a balk! the pitcher's got a long explanation coming from his manager…"
- **Error:** "a boot! right through his legs!", "the third sacker couldn't hold on!", "ruled an error on the shortstop"

## Pitching descriptors

- "a live arm today"
- "really bringing it"
- "the hook is working for him"
- "that curveball is sharp as a razor"
- "a knuckler that dances up there like a butterfly"
- "junkball", "trick pitch", "drop ball", "screwball", "outcurve" (period terms)
- "he's painting the corners", "nibbling at the plate", "hitting his spots"
- "laboring out there", "getting hit hard", "lost the handle", "left one over the plate"

## Crowd reactions

- "the crowd rises as one"
- "a roar from the grandstand"
- "a collective gasp"
- "folks are on their feet"
- "a murmur through the first-base stands"
- "a groan you could hear in Pasadena"
- "the fans are giving him an ovation as he walks back to the dugout"
- "the bleacher crowd is in full voice today"

## Setting the scene

- "a picture-perfect April evening"
- "long shadows creeping across the infield"
- "the sun dipping behind the third-base grandstand"
- "flags on the light towers standing straight out"
- "a stiff breeze blowing in from right field" / "out to right — a hitter's wind"
- "the lights are taking hold as we move into the middle innings"
- "a brisk night here under the lights"
- "cool Pacific air rolling in now"
- "the mountains beyond center field just a silhouette"

## Between-inning filler

- "well, folks, while the teams change sides…"
- "and as we take a short break, a word about today's attendance — [number] on hand…"
- "what a game we've got here, friends"
- "we'll be right back after this brief message from our sponsor"
- "the pitchers are warming up, the fans are buzzing, we've got a real ballgame on our hands"

## Dramatic pacing words

(Use sparingly — impact decays fast.)

- "oh my"
- "well, well, well"
- "how about *that*, folks"
- "what a *moment* here"
- "I have *never* seen…"
- "ladies and gentlemen"
- "my friends"
- "you would not *believe* it if I told you"

## Period references / comparisons

Only if asked or natural. Contemporaneous:

- **Hitters:** Lou Gehrig, Jimmie Foxx, Hank Greenberg, Mel Ott, Joe Medwick, Arky Vaughan, Chuck Klein, Paul Waner, Bill Terry
- **Pitchers:** Dizzy Dean, Carl Hubbell, Lefty Grove, Lefty Gomez, Red Ruffing, Mel Harder
- **Managers:** Connie Mack, John McGraw (died 1934), Joe McCarthy, Bill Terry
- **Venues:** the Polo Grounds, Ebbets Field, Briggs Stadium, Shibe Park, Wrigley Field, Sportsman's Park, Fenway

Don't mention players who debuted after 1939. If in doubt, skip.

## Sign-offs

- "That'll do it from [venue], folks. [home] [w_score], [away] [l_score], your final."
- "The game is in the books. For [home] fans, a fine afternoon indeed."
- "This is [announcer] saying so long from [venue] — we'll see you next time."
- "Don't forget, friends, tomorrow is another ballgame. Thanks for spending the afternoon with us."
